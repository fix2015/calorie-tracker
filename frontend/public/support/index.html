Support

Write to frontendappdevelopment@gmail.com. Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened, and which phone you use. We answer every message.

The calories for my meal look wrong

Photo estimates are approximate. Take the photo from above in good light with the whole plate visible. You can edit the items and portions before saving, or scan the barcode of packaged food for the values on the label.

How do I change my daily goal?

Open Profile and update your goal, weight or activity level. Your daily target is recalculated from those.

How do I change the language or units?

Both are in Profile. Calorize is available in English, German, Spanish, French, Polish and Ukrainian.

Someone posted something offensive

Tap the flag icon on the meal, comment, profile or message and choose a reason. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also block a user from their profile.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and all of your data. It cannot be undone.

I can't sign in

Check that you are using the same sign-in method as when you registered (email and password, Apple or Google). If it still fails, write to us from the email address of your account.

Is Calorize medical advice?

No. Calorize gives general nutrition estimates. Talk to a healthcare professional before making significant changes to your diet.
